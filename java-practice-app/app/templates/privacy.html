Privacy

Java Practice is a free practice site. This page explains in plain words what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your email address, to log you in, confirm your account and let you reset your password. If you opt in, we also use it for reminder and weekly recap emails, and we keep your timezone to time them.
  • A username (optional). It appears as @name on the achievement cards you choose to share. If you do not pick one we use the part of your email before the @; you can change it or hide your name on the account page. Anyone who has a shared card link can see it.
  • Leaderboard choice. Only if you turn it on in your account, your username and the number of questions you solved this week or all time are shown publicly in the top 10 (on the leaderboard and the login page).
  • A hashed password. We never store the password itself.
  • Your learning data: the level you chose, which questions you solved, the code you saved, your practice days, streaks, review queue and badges.
  • Usage events such as signing up, running code and finishing a day, so we can see whether the site works and improve it.
  • Issue reports you send, together with the code that was in your editor at the time.

Cookies and browser storage

  • A session cookie keeps you logged in.
  • A timezone cookie (tz) lets us count your day by your own clock.
  • Your browser also keeps a copy of your unsaved code, offline copies of pages you visited, and a small summary of your progress (streak, questions solved) for the "waking up" screen, on your own device. Logging out removes the summary.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who sees it

Only the site owner. We do not sell or share your data. Emails are sent through Gmail. The site runs on Render and the database on a hosted Postgres service, which store the data on our behalf.

Your code runs on our server inside a restricted process with no internet access. Please do not put secrets or personal data in your code.

Your control

From your account page you can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account and all of its data permanently.

Content note

Questions are written with AI assistance and may contain mistakes. Use the "Report an issue" button on any question to tell us.
